# 09 - Lore digest (for the story AI)

The Knox Event as Project Zomboid B42 tells it, summarised in our own words from the game's EN lore files (RadioData, Recorded_Media, Print_Media, Print_Text, Stash, DynamicRadio). A digest, not a copy. Tags: [R] radio/TV, [P] print, [V] VHS/CD, [S] stash notes, [A] automated military band.

Radio "day 0" = 9 July 1993 (the general's first statement is dated then); radio days and newspaper dates agree from there. On-air day counts of the lockdown are inconsistent; keep them fuzzy.

## 1. Timeline
**Before**
- 1-2 Jul: Knox Telecommunications phone/cell/internet outage, official "maintenance"; an expert suspects cut wires or sabotage [P]. Callers: lines died a week before the cordon [R].
- 3 Jul: overloaded Army truck with secret hazardous waste overturns near March Ridge; "no danger" [P].
- 4 Jul: Independence Day goes ahead. A caller later claims it began that day [R].
- 5 Jul: "unusual illness" in Muldraugh; foul smell over the region; spate of dog attacks [P]. Sick, aggressive dogs recur in many sources [R][V].
- 6 Jul: dozens with fever, vomiting, nausea; some bitten (a doctor suspects rabies). The Army opens its Fort Knox/March Ridge hospital; CDC en route; people use short-range radio [P].
- 6-7 Jul: overnight, troops fence off "Knox Country" with gear meant for overseas. Thousands trapped, no reason given [P][R].

**Lockdown**
- 9 Jul: Gen. John McGrew: unidentified flu-like outbreak causing "panic and confusion", no deaths, warning shots, no-fly zone, no questions. The President backs him. Relatives camp on the line; journalists who enter are detained [R].
- 10 Jul: McGrew apologises for a "lack of clarity". Army scientists (Jake Wilson, John Ramirez) say it has slowed and people will recover. DC protest turns violent; panic-buying, looting [R].
- 11 Jul: WHO advice grounds all non-military, non-medical flights. Troops enter US cities; the Zone is widened at night, camps trucked away [R][P].
- 12 Jul: the President takes blame, calls recovery less likely, sets 6 PM curfews. A leaked West Point photo: a one-armed, blank-faced man in a street of bodies [R][P].
- 13 Jul: civilians held in a base inside the Zone walk out (cells open, guards gone); people taken "for tests" never came back. McGrew admits it is degenerative, spread only by bites/scratches, sufferers "NOT deceased" [R][P].

**Collapse**
- 14 Jul: shooting and tear gas at the border camp draw a wave of infected out of the Zone (about 5 AM); up to ~100 dead. The Army retreats north. The President has left DC [R][P].
- 15 Jul: Louisville gridlocked; mobs attack the symptomatic. Unbitten people fall ill: spread without contact. Bridges being blocked [R][P].
- 16 Jul: Louisville overrun. CDC: airborne "aerial strain", a small minority immune. A caller reports a bridge blown with refugees on it. Cases in Ohio, England, Somalia, South Korea, Japan; Congress to Mount Weather; the Herald's farewell issue [R][P].
- 17 Jul on: McGrew's recording to the unaffected: bites still infect, shoot even family (soldiers hand out printed copies). More cities listed. Networks go to static or looped farewells; an Emergency Broadcast repeats: small groups, don't travel, cities lost, "we will come for you" [R]. The Knox grid fails in stages to blackout [A].

## 2. The infection
**Official line, in order:** flu-like, panic and confusion, no deaths -> slowing, recovery expected -> recovery receding -> degenerative, fluid contact only, not dead, cure sought -> airborne, few immune, cure near zero -> shoot them. Each step was contradicted.

**Witnesses:** fever, sleepiness, nausea, collapse; then death and return, dazed, violent, hungry. Infection inside the Zone said to be total. The dead feel no pain, shrug off body shots, fall to head wounds, follow noise and gunfire, form hordes. Bite or scratch = fever, death, rising; incubation "variable". The second wave (from 15 Jul) kills within about a day with no contact. A small minority resists it, never bites; survivors call them "the Immune" [R][P]. Survivor TV: boil water, burn bodies [R].

## 3. Origin: never settled
- Military lab: callers on labs in the Zone; Sun-Times asks if a military lab leaked; a home tape on a "restricted area" lab west of Rosewood with livestock deaths and bad tap water [R][P][V].
- 1960s/Vietnam-era chemical tests (denied or "classified"), toxic dumps, the waste truck [R][P].
- Pollution: polluted Ohio River, weeks-long smell, algae [R][P][V].
- Planned: the cordon rose too fast; a fired phone worker says lines were cut on orders [R].
- Mad Cow: Prof. Endsleigh's *Apocalypse Cow*, "it's in the meat", Spiffo's [R]. Spiffo's experimental sauce shipped to Kentucky (a joke) [P].
- Meteors seen over Kentucky [R]; a tape on 1992 Brazil meteors followed by smell, nausea, fever [V].
- Terrorism, God's judgment, crank calls (phone-line virus, Atlantis, CDC "Juniper-17", internment plan, no virus) [R].
- Spread abroad despite no flights: "everywhere all at once"? [R]
- Precedent: Raleigh EVNA outbreak (1992, North Carolina, blamed on government labs; CDC criticised) and a 1989 Libyan one [R][V].

## 4. Voices
- **WBLN News** (TV): anchor Diane; Richard Gershwin (detained, later bitten on air); *Talk the Night* with Dawn Stevenson. Earnest, then raw.
- **Triple-N** (TV, relayed by TURBO): Joan; Kirsty Cormick in the field; **Judge Matt Hass**, "the voice of reason", pro-Army, ends preaching guns and despair.
- **NNR Radio**: flat national bulletins and case lists; ends with Pastor James Hartnell, bitten, praying as the dead break in.
- **LBMW Kentucky Radio**: Jackie Jaye and Frank Hemingway, embedded then defiant; later survivor Jonas Underwood, who finds Jackie dead.
- **KnoxTalk Radio**: call-in show of evacuees, cranks, witnesses; signs off 17 Jul.
- **Knox Hitz / Hitz FM**: DJs Frankie, Janine, Billy; wry, dedications to people inside. **USR**: Don Beverage's crank call-in.
- **KPATV**: later, survivors Frank Williams, Maria, Quentin give survival tips and read out survivor names.
- Entertainment channels (GBC, Life and Living, PawsTV, NSTV, BMC) give way to curfew notices and reruns.
- **Amateur**: a local ham who stays inside the Zone and goes silent; an unknown voice inside the Zone (snipers, noise draws the dead); Hal, then Zach, inviting survivors to the Nashville Air National Guard base.
- **Military**: a numbers station; the automated "Fiver Zero Two" system (weather, grid alerts, garbled chatter: "Operation Artemis", "they need more data", a doubt about who sends the orders, contacts "from the East") [A].

## 5. Places and institutions
- **Knox Country / Event Zone**: counties around Fort Knox, below the Ohio, south of Louisville.
- **Muldraugh** (first cases), **West Point** (photo; Army bar town), **March Ridge** (hospital, truck, Cold War bunker for Fort Knox leaders), **Rosewood** (prison; rumoured lab), plus Riverside, Ekron, Irvington, Brandenburg, Echo Creek, Doe Valley.
- **Louisville**: border camps, falls 16 Jul. **Fort Knox**: Army Armor Center, gold depository. **Nashville ANG base**: radio enclave.
- Gen. John McGrew; the unnamed President; CDC; WHO; UN; FBI (Operation Centaur); Gov. Cal Fairweather.
- Knox Telecommunications, Spiffo's, Fossoil/Gas-2-Go, Knox Bank, Pizza Whirled, Greene's, Zippee, Crossroads Mall.
- Papers: Knox Knews, Kentucky Herald, Louisville Sun-Times, National Dispatch.

## 6. Motifs for a mystery
- Blackout: phones dead before the event, censored embedded press, cameras cut, leaked photos, "why weren't we told?"
- Soldiers as protectors and jailers; tests nobody returns from; guards gone overnight; unbitten people shot.
- Fear of the feverish; mobs; "the living are dangerous too".
- Sick dogs first; a foul smell for weeks.
- Voices cut off mid-sentence.
- Religion: judgment, doubt, a last prayer.
- Notes left behind: meet-me-heres, a loved one "is one of them", noise warnings, "one bite = death"; a Spiffo's manager who "knew what was coming" [S].

## 7. Gaps a story may fill
- The cause; whether the Army caused or only contained it.
- Operation Artemis; who sends the orders; "more data"; what came "from the East".
- The numbers station's meaning; the same digits sit on a Raleigh news tape [V].
- The truck's cargo; who cut the phones.
- What happened in the quarantine base and why the guards left.
- Why some are immune. The President's fate. Rosewood Prison. Whether Raleigh was a rehearsal.
- Whether Zach's Nashville group is safe: Hal's group was overrun and Zach "took over" (RadioData.xml labels that segment `kanibals`; no broadcast says so).

## 8. Do-not-contradict
- Knox Country, Kentucky, July 1993. Cordon 6-7 Jul; McGrew's first statement 9 Jul; Louisville falls 16 Jul.
- Phones down before the cordon; flights grounded 11 Jul; city curfews 12 Jul.
- Bites/scratches infect and kill; the dead rise; noise draws them; head wounds stop them.
- An airborne second wave killed most people; the survivors are the immune few; a bite still infects them.
- Worldwide; no cure; no rescue so far.
- Origin never confirmed: every theory stays a rumour.
- McGrew commands; the President is unnamed; power and broadcast media are gone; only automated, amateur and survivor signals remain.
